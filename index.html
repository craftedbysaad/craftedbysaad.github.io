I'm Muhammad Saad — a web and app designer from Sangla Hill, Pakistan, now based in Bahrain. I started with WordPress, HTML and CSS, and five years later I design and ship complete products on my own.

My work sits where design meets building: UI/UX, iOS and Android apps, custom-coded websites, and WordPress with a proper backend. I also do graphic design, photo and video editing, and photography — so the visuals are handled in-house too.

I use AI to build faster, but nothing goes out on autopilot. Every screen, animation and pixel is checked by hand. No templates. No AI slop.
